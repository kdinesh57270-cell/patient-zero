Delete your account and data

Last updated: 14 November 2026

You can delete your Patient Zero account and your data at any time. You do not need to keep the app installed.

Option 1 – inside the Game

  1. Open the Game and go to Settings (gear icon).
  2. Tap Delete account & data and confirm.

If you signed in with Google, you may be asked to sign in again before deletion, for safety.

Option 2 – by email

Send an email to kdineshkumar57270@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my account”, from the Google address you used to sign in (or include your Friend ID shown in the Game’s profile screen). We will delete your data and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your online account (Google sign-in link or guest ID) in Firebase Authentication.
  • Your saved profile on the device: nickname, age, coins, items and settings.
  • Room data (positions, chat) is temporary and removed when a room closes.

What may remain

  • Analytics data (only if you accepted analytics) is kept by Google for its standard retention period.
  • Information held by ad providers under their own policies, and data we must keep to meet legal duties or to handle abuse reports.

See also the Privacy Policy.
