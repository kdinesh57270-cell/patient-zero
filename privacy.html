Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the game Patient Zero (the “Game”) collects, how it is used and what choices you have. The Game is published by DEVELOPER_NAME_HERE (“we”, “us”). Contact: CONTACT_EMAIL_HERE.

1. Who can play

The Game is for people aged 13 or older. It is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 13 uses the Game, email us and we will delete the data.

2. Information we collect

  • Saved on your device: nickname, age, coins, items, character, settings, language, friend IDs and a list of blocked chat names. This stays in your browser/app storage and is not sent to us unless you play online.
  • Sign-in (Firebase Authentication, Google): if you choose “Sign in with Google”, the Game receives your name, email address, profile picture link and a unique user ID. If you continue as a guest, an anonymous user ID is created.
  • Online play (Firebase Realtime Database, hosted in Singapore): while you are in a room we process the room code, your user ID, nickname, in-game position and state, game events and chat messages. This room data is temporary and is deleted when the room is closed.
  • Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase) – optional: only if you tap “Accept analytics”. It may collect app/page usage, device and browser information, an approximate location derived from your IP address and online identifiers. If you decline, analytics are not started.
  • Ads: the Game shows ads from Google AdSense and Monetag. These providers may use cookies, device or advertising identifiers and your IP address to show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud (see section 4).
  • Technical data: your IP address and browser details are visible to the services that deliver the Game: our host (GitHub Pages), Google Fonts, Google Sign-In and the ad providers.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data.

3. How we use information

  • to run the Game, sign you in and connect you with other players;
  • to show chat and player names to others in the same room;
  • to keep the Game safe (moderation, abuse reports, blocking);
  • to understand and improve the Game (analytics, only if accepted);
  • to show and measure ads.

4. Ads, cookies and consent

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this Game. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings. To learn how Google uses data from sites or apps that use its services, see policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Monetag, another ad provider, handles data under its own privacy policy, which you can find on its website.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Google’s consent message asks for your choice before personalised ads are served. You can open the Game’s own choice for analytics at any time in Settings > Privacy choices.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is processed for us by service providers: Google (Firebase Authentication, Realtime Database, Analytics, AdSense, Fonts, Sign-In), Monetag (ads) and GitHub (hosting). Other players in your room can see your nickname and chat messages. Do not share personal details in chat.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Local data stays until you use Settings > Reset all data or clear your browser/app data.
  • Your online account (Google or guest) and local data are removed with Settings > Delete account & data. You can also ask by email or follow the steps on the Delete account page.
  • Room data (players, positions, chat) is temporary and deleted when the room is closed.
  • Analytics data, if you accepted it, is kept by Google according to its retention settings and cannot be removed by the Game itself.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or US state privacy laws) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent and to complain to your data protection authority. To use these rights, email CONTACT_EMAIL_HERE.

8. International transfers and security

Our servers and our providers’ services may be located outside your country (for example Singapore and the United States). Data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

9. Moderation

Chat is filtered for some words. Tap a chat message in a room to report it to us or block that player on your device. We may remove players or content that break the Terms of Use.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.
